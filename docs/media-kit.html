📣 About SpellBot

Everything on this page is free to use when writing about, streaming, or linking to SpellBot, as long as you follow the Terms of Service. Need something that isn't here? Email spellbot@lexicalunit.com.

If you need a short description, feel free to use this one:

SpellBot is the Discord bot for Webcam Magic. Run /lfg in a Discord server and SpellBot gathers the players and then opens the table on Convoke.

🌈 Colors

SpellBot's signature is the gradient from violet to amber that runs through the d20 in the logo. On screen it's drawn at 45°, from amber in the bottom left to violet in the top right.

  • Violet #5A3EFD Primary. The top of the d20.
  • Amber #F6AD4B Secondary. The bottom of the d20.
  • Bright violet #7C5CFF Violet lifted for UI and thin lines on dark backgrounds.
  • Ground #1A202C Background.
  • White #FFFFFF Text on the ground color.

📏 Guidelines

  • Leave clear space around the logo of at least a quarter of its height.
  • Scale the logo proportionally. Don't stretch, rotate, or recolor it, and don't add effects such as shadows or outlines.
  • Pick the version that suits the background: the full-color logo or a light wordmark on dark backgrounds, a dark wordmark on light ones.
  • Don't use the logo in a way that suggests SpellBot sponsors or endorses you or your product.
  • Write the name as SpellBot: one word, with a capital S and a capital B.
